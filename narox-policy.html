NAROX Privacy Policy

NAROX may temporarily collect and store limited data such as:

  • User IDs
  • Server IDs
  • Moderation data
  • Leveling data
  • Activity-related data

This data is used strictly for the functionality and operation of the bot.

NAROX does not sell, trade, or share user data with third parties.

Users may request deletion of their stored data by contacting the developer on Discord: ihatenarox
